Understanding Autism

Before you begin the screening process, it is essential to understand exactly what we are looking at. Autism is not a disease, and it is not a disorder that needs to be "cured."

It is a form of neurodivergence a natural variation in the human brain regarding how it processes information, socializes, and perceives the environment. Individuals on the autism spectrum simply experience the world through a different, often highly detailed and unique, neurological lens.

This toolkit is designed to provide clarity and understanding, leveraging AI to help map these unique neurological patterns so individuals can get the support and environment they need to thrive.
